PRIVACY POLICY

Plain language, no legal maze. Here's exactly what happens to your data when you visit The Engine, what cookies do, and how advertising on this site works.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

The Engine ("we," "us," "this site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, why it's collected, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who runs this site

The Engine is an independently run tribute site covering engine history ; engineering and car culture, plus the Drift Lab game and Engine Builder tool. It isn't affiliated with any car manufacturer named on these pages the Ford, Ferrari, Bugatti and other names you'll see are used for editorial and identification purposes only. You can reach the person behind it on the Contact page.

2. Information we collect

Automatically collected data

Like almost every website, some technical information is logged automatically when you visit: approximate location at country or city level (from your IP address), browser and device type, pages viewed, time spent on the site, and the page that referred you here. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, covered in Section 3.

Information you choose to give us

If you use the contact form or email us directly, we receive whatever you include usually your email address and your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any mailing list without asking first.

Drift Lab & Engine Builder

High scores from Drift Lab and any engine configuration you build in the Engine Builder are stored locally in your own browser. None of that data is sent to us or to any server we control.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for three things: remembering your cookie-consent choice, basic anonymous analytics on which pages get read, and displaying advertising as described below. On your first visit. A banner lets you accept or decline nonessential cookies you can change your mind at any time by clearing cookies for this site or adjusting your browser's settings.

4. Advertising Google AdSense & partners

This site is monetized through Google AdSense and may work with other third-party advertising networks over time. These partners can use cookies, web beacons and similar technology to learn about your visits to this and other sites, so they can show ads that are more relevant to you.

  • Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites across the web.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising any time through Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info/choices.
  • For the full detail on how Google uses data from partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

Advertising partners are third parties with their own privacy policies we don't control the cookies they set so it's worth checking their policies directly if you want the full technical picture.

5. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the site
  • To understand which pages and tools like Drift Lab and the Builder actually get used
  • To serve relevant advertising, which is what keeps the site free
  • To reply to anything sent through the contact form

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising partners process data under their own terms, as described in Section 4.

6. Your rights

If you're in the EU or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw cookie consent at any time. To exercise any of these, get in touch via the Contact page.

If you're in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information which under California law can include the use of advertising cookies. You can do this by declining non-essential cookies in the consent banner, or through the Google Ads Settings link above.

7. Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information collected through this site, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in practice or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly. Continuing to use the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through the Contact page.
