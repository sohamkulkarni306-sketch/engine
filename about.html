The Engine started as a rabbit hole about a 19th century grocery salesman and turned into a full site. Here's the story, and who's actually behind it.

Why this site exists

It started with one question: why does every car engine on the road, from a base-model hatchback to a Bugatti, ultimately trace back to the same four-stroke idea from 1876? Once I started reading about Nicolaus Otto and how his engine splintered into everything from the inline-four to the V12, it was hard to stop. The Engine is that research turned into something worth sharing an actual reference for anyone curious about how engines got the way they are, not just another spun-content listicle.

Everything on the History, Evolution and Cars pages was researched and written page by page, checked against public sources, and rewritten until it actually reads like something a person wanted to tell you not like it was generated to hit a word count. Drift Lab and the Engine Builder came later, once it was clear the history alone wasn't going to scratch the itch to actually build and drive something.

Who's behind it

The Engine is written, designed and maintained independently it isn't a corporate content farm or an auto manufacturer's marketing page. It's not affiliated with Ford, Ferrari, Bugatti, Nissan, Subaru or any other brand mentioned across the site; those names show up purely because their engines are part of the story being told.
